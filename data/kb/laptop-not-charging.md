# Laptop not charging on a dock or USB-C charger

## Check the basics
1. Plug the charger straight into the laptop instead of the dock. If it charges, the dock is the problem.
2. Try a different USB-C port on the laptop. On ThinkPads, use the port nearest the hinge.
3. Look at the charger wattage. Northwind docks deliver 90 W; a 45 W phone charger cannot charge a laptop under load.

## Reset the dock
Unplug the dock's power for 30 seconds, plug it back in, then reconnect the laptop.

## When to raise a ticket
If the laptop does not charge from its own charger either, it needs a hardware check. Raise a ticket with your asset tag so the hardware team can swap the charger or book a repair.
