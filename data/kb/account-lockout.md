# Account locked after failed sign-ins

## Why it happens
Ten failed sign-ins in 15 minutes lock the account for 30 minutes. A phone or tablet with an old password saved is the most common cause.

## Fix
Wait 30 minutes or unlock it from the self-service portal with MFA. Then update the password on every device that syncs mail.
