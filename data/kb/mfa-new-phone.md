# Move MFA to a new phone

## Before you start
You need your laptop signed in, or a one-time code from the help desk.

## Steps
1. On your laptop, open the self-service portal and choose **Security > Authenticator app > Replace device**.
2. Install the Northwind Authenticator on the new phone and scan the QR code shown in the portal.
3. Approve the test prompt on the new phone. The old phone stops receiving prompts.

## Lost your old phone
If you no longer have the old phone and are signed out everywhere, the help desk can issue a one-time code after verifying you on a video call.
