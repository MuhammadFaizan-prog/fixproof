# Encrypted USB drives for client handover

## Policy
Company-issued encrypted USB drives (the grey Kingston IronKey) may be used to hand files to a client when a secure share link is not possible. Unlock the drive with its PIN, copy only the files the client needs, and log the handover in the client folder.
