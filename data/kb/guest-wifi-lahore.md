# Guest Wi-Fi in the Lahore office

## Steps
The Lahore office has no reception desk. Visitors join NW-Guest-LHE and the hosting employee requests a guest code from the IT channel in Teams; the code lasts for the length of the visit. The NW-Guest one-day codes do not work in Lahore.
