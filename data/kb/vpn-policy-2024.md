# VPN client troubleshooting policy (2024)

## Policy
If the VPN keeps dropping and a profile reset does not help, uninstall the VPN client and reinstall it from the software portal. Employees may reinstall the client themselves.

## Notes
Reinstalling clears cached certificates. Expect to sign in again with MFA.
