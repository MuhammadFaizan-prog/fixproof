# Lahore office network and power backup

## Notes
During scheduled power load-shedding, the Lahore office runs on generator power and the main switch restarts once. Wi-Fi returns within two minutes. If the VPN drops at the same time, reconnect after the Wi-Fi is back rather than resetting the profile.
