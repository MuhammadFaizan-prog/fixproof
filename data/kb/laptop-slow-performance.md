# Laptop is slow or behaving strangely

## First steps
1. Restart the laptop. Many issues clear after a full restart, not just closing the lid.
2. Check free disk space. Less than 10 GB free slows every device down.
3. Install pending updates from the software portal.

## Tell us what you see
"Slow" or "weird" can mean many things. A useful ticket says what you were doing, what happened, and when it started, for example "Excel freezes for 10 seconds when I open shared files, since Monday".

## Possible security issue
Unexpected pop-ups, new browser toolbars or files you did not create can be signs of malware. Disconnect from the network and contact the help desk by phone.
