# Add a floor printer on macOS

## Steps
1. Open **System Settings > Printers & Scanners** and click **Add Printer**.
2. Choose the printer named after your floor, for example "NW-LDN-3F".
3. Select **Northwind Secure Print** as the driver and click **Add**.
Print jobs are held until you tap your badge at the printer.
