# Reset your password

## Steps
1. Go to the self-service portal and choose **Forgot password**.
2. Approve the MFA prompt, then choose a new password of at least 14 characters.
3. On a Mac, update the password in **Keychain Access** when prompted.
The help desk never asks for your password.
