# Outlook for Mac not syncing

## Fix
1. Check you are online and not in **Work Offline** mode (Outlook menu).
2. Go to **Outlook > Settings > Accounts**, select your account and choose **Repair**.
3. If mail is still missing, remove the account and add it again with your work email.
