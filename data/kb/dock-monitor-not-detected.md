# External monitor not detected on the dock

## Fix
1. Check the monitor is on the right input (DisplayPort or HDMI).
2. Unplug the laptop from the dock and plug it back in.
3. On Windows press **Win + P** and choose **Extend**. On a Mac open **System Settings > Displays** and click **Detect Displays** while holding Option.
