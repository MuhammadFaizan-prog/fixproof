# Mac says the disk is almost full

## Fix
1. Open **System Settings > General > Storage** and review the recommendations.
2. Empty the Trash and the Downloads folder.
3. Turn on **Optimize Mac Storage** for OneDrive files so they stay in the cloud until opened.
