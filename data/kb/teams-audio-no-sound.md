# Teams calls with no sound

## Fix
1. In Teams, open **Settings > Devices** and choose the right speaker and microphone.
2. Make a test call from the same page.
3. Unplug and replug the headset, then select it again.
4. On a Mac, check **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone** allows Teams.
